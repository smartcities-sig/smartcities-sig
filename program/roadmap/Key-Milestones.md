## Smart Cities SIG at Smart City Expo World Congress 2026

Since its launch, the SmartCities Special Interest Group (SIG) has worked toward a major milestone: delivering key analytical insights on public street lighting and water management.
Our research identified:
- Essential capabilities municipalities need for urban management
- Data requirements for Digital Twins
- Existing open standards and open source initiatives addressing these needs, and where critical gaps remain

We presented these early results live in Barcelona at Smart City Expo World Congress 2026 (3–4 November), at the Madrid Municipality stand. The slides from each session are available below.

## What's Next
The sessions in Barcelona presented early results. The SIG is still refining the methodology and developing the public street lighting and water management profiles, using the feedback gathered at the Expo. Updates will be shared as the work progresses.

If your city, organization or project would like to follow the work, share feedback or contribute, join the conversation on the SmartCities SIG Community Page.
